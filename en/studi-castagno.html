Feasibility study

The chestnut of the future

A feasibility study in the chestnut sector and governance policies for the quality agri-food supply chain of Campania's chestnuts and marrons. Prototyping of technological systems for agriculture 4.0.

The remarkable world of the chestnut groves of the Apennines and the Prealps, and of Campania's in particular, now needs an extraordinary effort to save a unique ecosystem.

Campania's chestnut growing, in particular, has been — and must return to being — a value that is not only economic but also ecological and social. Campania must promote a sustainable and responsible investment that sets an example for the rest of the country.

The chestnut sector is a multidisciplinary one, where the sustainability of a territory and its resilience come together: it is truly a unique opportunity to demonstrate how a sustainable and responsible investment can be designed, capable of integrating financial-economic analysis with environmental-ecological analysis. Especially in view of the ongoing climate change and the importance of Campania's water resources.

"An extraordinary tangible and intangible heritage is perhaps one of the most respectful definitions of Italian chestnut growing: one made of maintenance and prevention of hydrogeological instability, of the integration of traditional and innovative agricultural practices, of multiple industrial transformations, of a potentially rich supply chain, of landscape, of biodiversity protection, of a provider of ecosystem services of incalculable value, and also one made of narrative, stories, legends and gastronomy. And all this also emerges from the study presented. This work lays the foundations for the next pages in the glorious history of Campania's chestnut growing."

— Antonio De Cristofaro, President of the Consorzio Distretto della Castagna e del Marrone della Campania · Full Professor, Department of Agriculture, Environment and Food, University of Molise

The chestnut: from the bread tree, a nutraceutical resource.

In recent years, scientific research on chestnuts has produced numerous findings that support their importance and their complementary role in the human diet.

Information is available covering both basic nutritional data and more targeted findings, which reveal the presence of bioactive compounds and their enzymatic metabolites: factors that underline the importance of including chestnuts in a balanced diet.
