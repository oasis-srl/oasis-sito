03
Biological control

The natural antagonist of the chestnut gall wasp.

OASIS supplies specimens of Torymus sinensis, the hymenopteran antagonist of the chestnut gall wasp (Dryocosmus kuriphilus). The service includes supply, release protocol, post-release monitoring and technical assistance.

  • ✓
