About us

Twenty years of research at the service of business.

OASIS was founded in 2004 from the idea of a group of researchers and professionals convinced that business consultancy should rest on solid scientific foundations, not merely on administrative expertise.

Our identity

Three vocations that walk together.

OASIS S.r.l. is a research and consultancy company that has operated since 2004 with three complementary, formally recognised roles:

Research Organisation without profit aims, recognised under EU Communication 2006/C 323/01 — the results of our research activities are intended for the dissemination of knowledge, not for profit.

Innovative SME, registered in the special Register at the Chamber of Commerce, meeting the requirements set out in Legislative Decree 3/2015.

Operational Research and Development Centre, with in-house technical and scientific staff, separate accounting for research activities and a management system certified to ISO 9001 and ISO 14001.

The combination of these three roles allows us to support businesses in a unique way: we design the research activity, carry it out, document it, and then assist the company in accessing the subsidised finance instruments that support it.

The milestones

From a team of researchers to a recognised Research Organisation.

2004

OASIS S.r.l. is founded

A group of researchers and business consultants founds OASIS with the aim of combining scientific research and subsidised finance.

2009

Launch of the Torymus programme

The first Italian activities in the biological control of the chestnut gall wasp begin, using the natural antagonist Torymus sinensis.

2010

First research projects funded from public sources

The first applied research projects funded from public sources begin, with OASIS as scientific partner.

2018

SMAU Innovation Award for the POSSIBILE project

OASIS receives the SMAU Innovation Award in Milan for the POSSIBILE project, on biostimulants for organic agriculture funded under Horizon 2020.

2018

Registration in the Register of Innovative SMEs

OASIS obtains recognition as an Innovative SME, which certifies its strong orientation towards research and development activity.

2021

ISO 9001 and 14001 certifications

The quality and environmental management systems are certified by an accredited body, formalising processes that had already been operating internally for years.

2024

Recognition as a Research Organisation

The completion of the journey: OASIS is formally a Research Organisation pursuant to EU Communication 2006/C 323/01.
