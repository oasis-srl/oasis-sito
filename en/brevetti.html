A share of the results we produce is consolidated into patent titles filed with the Italian Patent and Trademark Office (UIBM). They are the most concrete proof that the research we design for ourselves and with our partners reaches an original and industrially applicable result.

Our patents

Three titles, three strands of research.

Two industrial inventions and one utility model, filed together with partner companies, CNR researchers and university professors.

Agro-sciences · Crop protection Industrial invention

IDRO-BIO — Nematicidal biofungicide with biofumigant-action micro-organisms

A crop-protection formulation that harnesses micro-organisms capable of releasing biofumigant compounds, to contain pathogenic fungi and nematodes without resorting to synthetic chemistry.

Agri-food · Preservation Industrial invention

Biopolymer-based systems for food preservation
