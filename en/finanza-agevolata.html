What it covers

The complete service, with no fragmentation.

We cover the entire life cycle of the application, from the first feasibility study through to the final financial reporting and any dispute proceedings with the Italian Revenue Agency.

01

Feasibility study

We check whether your project is compatible with the chosen funding instrument, before you invest time and resources.

02

Drafting the application

We write the proposal with the required technical and scientific documentation, in line with the standards of the awarding body.

03

Technical sworn certification

For the R&D tax credit we produce the sworn technical report required by the regulations.

04

Application management

Liaison with the awarding body, supplementary submissions, clarifications, deadline monitoring.

05

Final financial reporting

Expenditure documentation, final technical report, financial statement — all compliant with the grant call requirements.

06

Support in the event of audits

If an audit or dispute proceedings arise, we stand alongside the company with documentation already prepared and defensible.

Who it is for

Manufacturing

Manufacturing SMEs

Agriculture

Agricultural and agro-industrial businesses

Rural Development Programme (PSR) grant calls, specific measures for the chestnut sector, precision agriculture, organic farming, short supply chains.

Innovation

Start-ups and innovative SMEs

Companies with ongoing R&D activity that want to structure access to the tax credit as a recurring instrument.
