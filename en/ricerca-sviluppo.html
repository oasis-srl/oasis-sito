The regulatory framework

What it means to be a Research Organisation — and how we apply it.

A Research Organisation, under EU Communication 2006/C 323/01 and the subsequent Framework for State aid for research and development and innovation, is an entity that carries out research on a non-economic basis — whose results are not intended for profit but for the dissemination of knowledge.

For companies that collaborate with a Research Organisation, this means greater robustness of R&D tax credit claims, access to grant calls that require partnership with an R.O., and documentation compliant with European standards.

The three key requirements of an R.O.

1. Dissemination of results. The knowledge produced must be published and shared, not kept confidential for economic purposes.

2. Separate accounting. Research activities must be clearly distinguishable from any economic activities.

3. Scientific autonomy. The direction of research activities is independent of the commercial interests of the funders.

— EU Communication 2006/C 323/01
Our governance

From the rules to operating practice.

Being a Research Organisation is not a label: OASIS has adopted an internal charter approved by the shareholders' meeting on 31/03/2024, which translates those three principles into verifiable internal obligations.

01

Documented separate accounting

A separate accounting system distinguishes research activities from economic ones, ensuring the traceability of costs, revenues and funding for each category.

02

Profits reinvested in research

The company's profits are allocated to R&D activities and cannot be distributed among the shareholders: the aim is not profit but the dissemination of knowledge.

03

Ring-fenced public funding

Public funding is allocated exclusively to R&D activities, in compliance with Article 107(1) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union.

04

Scientific & Technical Committee

A committee of at least five members, including a university professor, oversees the independence of research activities and meets at least once a year.

05

Dissemination of results

The results of non-economic activities are disseminated through scientific publications, outreach events and open-access platforms.

06

Compliance Officer

An R.O. Compliance Officer periodically verifies the application of the charter, which is subject to annual review.

How this translates into a service for SMEs

Six ways we can support you.

Our role as a Research Organisation is not abstract: it translates into a concrete service for companies that want to structure their R&D activity, access the tax credit, or take part in grant calls that require accredited scientific partners.

01

Scientific design

Definition of the research hypothesis, review of the state of the art, identification of the methodology and expected outputs.

02

Delivery of activities

Our in-house researchers can carry out parts of the project directly, under a research contract.

03

Technical and scientific documentation

Project reports compliant with European legislation, defensible in the event of a review or audit.

04

Role as R.O. partner

When a grant call requires a Research Organisation among the project partners, OASIS can take on that role.

05

Relations with public bodies and universities

Activation of scientific collaborations with university departments, CNR, ENEA — when additional specialist expertise is needed.

06

Dissemination of results

Publications, conference contributions, outreach material — as required of a Research Organisation and useful for positioning the project.
