30 September 2026 Irpinia TV Interview

The Partenio chestnut earns PGI status

In an interview with Irpinia TV, filmed among the chestnut groves of Summonte (AV), Davide Della Porta — vice-president of the mark's promoting committee — commented on the PGI recognition of the Partenio chestnut. A milestone for the province of Avellino: after Montella and Serino, the Partenio too has earned its Protected Geographical Indication, covering an area of 22 municipalities.

Aired on Irpinia TV.

25–27 September 2026 OASIS × Energinova Team building

OASIS and Energinova joint retreat, in Palermo and Mondello

From 25 to 27 September 2026, in Palermo and Mondello, OASIS S.r.l. and Energinova S.r.l. held a joint retreat. Working sessions addressed the status of ongoing projects and shared development prospects, alongside team-building activities. The meeting strengthened the collaboration between the two companies.

10–12 September 2026 EuroCastanea Conference

OASIS at the 16th European Chestnut Days (EuroCastanea 2026)

OASIS S.r.l., represented by Davide Della Porta, took part in the 16th European Chestnut Days (EuroCastanea), held from 10 to 12 September 2026 in Carrazedo de Montenegro, Valpaços (Portugal). These were three days of European exchange on chestnut growing, gall-wasp control and supply-chain innovation.

Event info: eurocastanea.eu

13 May 2026 Telenostra TV feature

Torymus sinensis against the chestnut gall wasp

A research company in Torre le Nocelle develops a protection system to tackle the chestnut gall wasp: Torymus, an insect resembling an ant, intercepts and eats the larvae's eggs. Production down by 15 per cent.

Feature broadcast on Telenostra, a local television station.

Davide Della Porta receives the award for merit in agricultural science and technology
