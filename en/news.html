13 May 2026 Telenostra TV feature

Torymus sinensis against the chestnut gall wasp

A research company in Torre le Nocelle develops a protection system to tackle the chestnut gall wasp: Torymus, an insect resembling an ant, intercepts and eats the larvae's eggs. Production down by 15 per cent.

Feature broadcast on Telenostra, a local television station.

Davide Della Porta receives the award for merit in agricultural science and technology

Our Chief Technology Officer Davide Della Porta receives the National Award for Merit in Agricultural, Agri-food and Environmental Science and Technology — a recognition of the applied-research work carried out by OASIS in the agricultural and environmental sector.
