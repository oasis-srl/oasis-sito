Scientific outreach

The knowledge we produce belongs to everyone.

As a Research Organisation, outreach is not a secondary activity for us: it is a constituent part of our mandate. We publish in technical and scientific journals, take part in national and European conferences, and train agronomists, technicians and farmers. Because research, to be truly useful, must be given back to the community.

Our philosophy

Why outreach is a way of working.

For a consulting firm, accumulated knowledge is a competitive advantage to be guarded. For us it is the opposite: method and results are meant to be published, taken to conferences, taught in courses. A protocol that no one can verify is not research. It is also the reason we chose the path of the Research Organisation.

For every project we carry out, part of the value lies in giving back to the sector — chestnut growers, SMEs, public research — what we have learned.
