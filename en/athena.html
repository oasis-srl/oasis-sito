@thena: cardiology telemedicine with IoT and artificial intelligence.

An IoT system supported by AI-based monitoring and predictive models, serving telemedicine, remote control and remote assistance for cardiac patients.

The project

Secondary prevention for cardiac patients.

Cardiovascular disease is the leading cause of death in Italy. People who survive a cardiac event often live with a chronic condition that requires continuous monitoring and strong adherence to treatment and a healthy lifestyle.

@thena addresses the secondary prevention phase. It is developing a telemedicine platform that uses wearable devices and IoT sensors to collect cardiac patients' vital and biometric signs in real time and send them to the cardiologist. Using artificial intelligence, the data are analysed to detect critical situations early and enable timely intervention, including at home.

OASIS's role

Personalised nutrition and integrated rehabilitation.

Within the partnership, OASIS carries out the research on personalised nutrition for patients with heart failure and on rehabilitation support.

01

Ready-to-eat dishes

Development of three model ready-to-eat dishes (fifth range) designed around the nutritional needs of patients with heart failure.

02

Raw materials

Characterisation of the raw materials used, supporting the nutritional and health value of the preparations.

03

Meal plans

Design of personalised meal plans and high-health-value recipes for use by medical staff.

04

Physical and psychological support

Definition of an integrated physical and psychological support approach to make patient rehabilitation more effective.

Project objectives
  • Build an IoT system for continuous monitoring of parameters such as ECG, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, body composition, NT-proBNP and electrolytes.
  • Develop predictive AI models and natural-language-processing tools to interpret televisit and telemonitoring reports.
  • Study Point-of-Care devices for home use and an ion-selective-electrode medical device for measuring molecular markers.
  • Integrate clinical monitoring with a personalised care pathway: dedicated meal plans and protocols supporting physical activity and psychological well-being.
Expected results
  • A telemedicine platform for integrated home care and patient follow-up, with its cloud infrastructure.
  • Validated clinical datasets and artificial-intelligence models, documented and released under a licence.
  • Prototype devices for home monitoring of biomarkers.
  • Personalised meal plans and high-health-value recipes for patients with heart failure, together with integrated protocols for physical and psychological rehabilitation.
