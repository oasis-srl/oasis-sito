2. What data we collect and why

2.1 Data submitted through the contact form

By filling in the form on the Contact page, you provide us with: full name, company, role (optional), email, phone (optional), type of request and the content of your message.

Purpose: to respond to your request for information or consulting and to manage any pre-contractual relationship.
Legal basis: the performance of pre-contractual measures taken at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for the optional data, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
Retention: for the time needed to handle the request and in any case no longer than 24 months from the last contact, unless a contractual relationship is established.
Nature of provision: the fields marked with an asterisk are necessary in order to reply to you; without them the request cannot be handled.

2.2 Browsing data

The computer systems hosting the website acquire, during their normal operation, certain technical data whose transmission is implicit in the use of Internet protocols (IP address, browser type, time of the request, page visited). This data is used only to ensure the correct functioning of the website and for security purposes (Art. 6(1)(f) — legitimate interest) and is not associated with identified users.

3. Recipients and data processors

The data may be processed by authorised OASIS S.r.l. personnel and by the following providers, acting as data processors or as independent controllers:

  • GitHub, Inc. (Microsoft) — website hosting via GitHub Pages. Processes the browsing data referred to in point 2.2. GitHub privacy notice
  • FormSubmit — the service that forwards contact-form messages to our mailbox. The data passes through its servers only for the time needed for forwarding. FormSubmit privacy notice
  • Google LLC — loading of typefaces via Google Fonts. The browser transmits your IP address to Google in order to download the fonts. Google privacy notice
  • YouTube (Google LLC) — the Projects and Torymus pages show previews of videos hosted on YouTube. The preview images are served by our own website, so no data is transmitted to Google when the page loads. The player is embedded only if you choose to start playback, via the youtube-nocookie.com domain: from that point Google's privacy notice applies.

Some of these providers are based in the United States: the transfer takes place on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or of standard contractual clauses.

The data is not disclosed to other parties or disseminated, except where required by law.

4. Cookies

This website does not use profiling cookies or statistical analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics). No first-party cookies are installed. The third-party services listed in point 3 may use technical cookies in accordance with their respective notices; the YouTube videos are not embedded when the pages load, so no YouTube cookie is installed until you start playback.

5. Your rights

At any time you may exercise the rights provided by Articles 15–22 GDPR: access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent (without affecting the lawfulness of previous processing). To exercise them, write to segreteria@oasis-srl.it.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it).

6. Changes

This notice may be updated; the date of the latest revision is shown above. We invite you to consult it periodically.
